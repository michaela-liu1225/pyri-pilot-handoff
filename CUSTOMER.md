# Customer brief

This is an invented pilot customer and role, with no confidential customer data.

**User:** A forestry duty lead with a few minutes to review a shift briefing and limited time to chase sources. The next shift may have different staff. Reports can be late, contradictory in scope or missing.

**Outcome:** An incoming duty lead can identify what needs attention, understand what evidence they can trust, see what still needs verification, and leave a handoff another operator can understand. The user remains responsible for action.

**Existing product:** Pine Ridge demonstrates the working briefing/handoff journey. Upper Alder has been added to the registry but not onboarded into that journey. Preserve working functionality while extending the system.

## Requests arriving together

- Operations: “Can the next shift see what changed and what we still need to verify?”
- Account lead: “Can this work for the new reserve without a custom rebuild for every site?”
- Sales: “A map with every layer would look compelling.”
- Support: “When a feed fails, users should still understand what they can trust.”
- Future customer: “Could we add alerts and multiple users?”

Choose a small release around the required journey in README.md. Explain what you deferred and why. Rich mapping, alerts and multi-user features are options for the roadmap, not additional requirements. State one observable measure of success for your chosen user journey.
