All samples are in the ZIP and exposed as JSON by the starter. No remote sensing download, account, GIS package or raster processing is required. CSV numeric fields arrive as strings; an empty value means missing, not zero. All replay times are UTC.

## What is actually in the satellite CSVs

Each satellite product has **2 scenes x 1,280 sample points**. We read the source pixels nearest a common 40 x 32 sampling grid over the reserve's bounding box (roughly 385 x 396 m spacing). These are sparse point samples, **not area averages, complete imagery, or new 385 m-resolution measurements**. Some centers lie outside the AOI polygon. Keep the difference between native resolution, product grid and provided sampling density clear; mask spatially if your claim requires AOI-only statistics.

- Sentinel-2 processing baseline N0300: reflectance = DN / 10,000. NDVI = (NIR - red) / (NIR + red), supplied only for SCL classes 4/5/6 with positive bands. Raw reflectances remain available where the derived index is masked. This simple mask does not establish smoke-free data.
- Landsat ST_B10: Kelvin = DN x 0.00341802 + 149; Celsius = Kelvin - 273.15. Temperature is blank for zero DN or QA_PIXEL bits 0-5 (fill/cloud/cirrus/shadow/snow). The pack does not include every uncertainty band; do not imply validated operational accuracy.
- `quality` is a preparation flag, not a guaranteed truth label. Preserve missingness and inspect scene coverage before summarizing. We provide source IDs, acquisition times, unsigned asset URLs, methods and checksums in `data/multisource/provenance.json`.

## Possible combinations — choose your own

1. **Coverage-aware handoff:** local sensor trend + a selected satellite scene + reports. Show where evidence is recent, stale, delayed or missing, and what the next shift should verify.
2. **Reserve/access briefing:** a selected satellite or point-sensor feed plus verified access reports, with a toy assessment if useful. Explain what the observations do and do not establish about the reserve or the route. Other sources can be excluded on relevance or quality grounds.
3. **Comparison view:** optical or thermal background + sensor time series, with different timestamps and spatial support kept visible. Explain disagreement; do not invent a causal model that combines surface temperature, air temperature and fire detection into a validated risk score.

Implement at least two supplied evidence sources, including at least one satellite or point-sensor feed in the working result, and compare satellite versus point-sensor usefulness in your rationale. You need not use every source, rebuild the supplied map, process original rasters or invent a scientific prediction. Individual source exclusions are valid; excluding both satellite and point-sensor feeds from the working result would miss the required scope. Optional external sources may supplement this baseline under the rules in README.md. Prioritize a useful, deployed workflow within four hours.
