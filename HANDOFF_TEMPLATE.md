# Submission handoff

Keep this short; bullets are enough. Include it in your submitted source.

1. **User and outcome:** What should the incoming duty lead notice, trust and verify? What can they now do, and what observable measure would show it is useful?
2. **Scope and decisions:** Which two or more supplied evidence sources did you implement? Identify the satellite or point-sensor feed used in the working result. Compare satellite versus point-sensor resolution, freshness/availability, quality and usefulness; explain included/excluded layers and deferred work.
3. **Your contribution:** What did the starter already do, what did you change, and which files/functions implement the change? Identify the most consequential behaviour you independently verified.
4. **Run and review:** Demo URL, private login-sharing instructions, run/deploy/test commands, and the site/cutoff/failure case to demonstrate. Explain how the modified application handles failure and preserves saved evidence.
5. **Evidence and checks:** Trace one important displayed claim in your addition to its source, transformation and a test. Note known incorrect or unfinished behaviour and limitations.
6. **AI and process:** Tools used, one suggestion/output or assumption you independently checked, how you checked it, and what you changed or rejected. Full private prompt histories are not required.
7. **Optional additional data (if used):** For each source, give its URL/identifier, licence/attribution, observation/validity and availability times, retrieval date, resolution/support, transformations and limitations. State any historical-availability assumption, caching/retrieval instructions and failure fallback. What user need did it address, and how did you verify the improvement? If unused, simply say so.
8. **Time:** Actual elapsed work time, including discovery, data preparation, deployment and presentation preparation, and any setup blocker. Stop at four hours.
9. **Next commercial milestone:** The most important gap before a paid pilot, a concrete next step and why it beats another request. Explain how a third site would fit.

Do not put credentials in this file or in Git. Share demo access privately in the submission email.
